# Rishith's Email Style Guide

## Tone
- Direct, no fluff
- Professional but not stiff
- Gets to the point in the first sentence
- Short sentences, one idea per sentence

## Greeting
- Formal: "Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],"
- Semi-formal: "Hi Mr./Ms. [Last Name],"
- Casual: "Hi [First Name],"
- Never: "I hope this email finds you well"

## Structure
- Greeting
- Purpose of email (1 sentence)
- Details (2-3 short sentences max)
- Ask or closing
- Signature

## Sign-off
- Formal/scout: "YIS," then full name
- Casual: "Thanks," or just name
- Never: "Best regards," "Sincerely," "Looking forward to hearing from you"

## What he does
- Uses "Thank you for..." a lot
- Uses "Please" for requests
- Says "I apologize" when needed (not "I'm sorry")
- Direct asks: "Could you please...", "When you have a moment..."
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences)
- Proper punctuation

## What he never does
- No flattery ("I'm impressed by...")
- No emojis
- No exclamation marks (maybe one per email max)
- No "I hope this email finds you well"
- No long paragraphs
- No "Looking forward to your response"
- No "Please don't hesitate to reach out"
- No "I wanted to reach out because..."
